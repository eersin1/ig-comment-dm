This app is run by [YOUR NAME] (@[YOUR_INSTAGRAM_HANDLE]) for their own Instagram account only. It does two things:

1. When someone comments a keyword on one of @[YOUR_INSTAGRAM_HANDLE]'s reels, it sends that person one direct message with the resource promised in the reel, and may reply to the comment.
2. It lets @[YOUR_INSTAGRAM_HANDLE] post reels to their own account.

## What data is used

When you comment on a reel by @[YOUR_INSTAGRAM_HANDLE], the app reads your public comment through Meta's Instagram API: your username, the comment text, the time it was posted and its ID. It does not access your profile, your followers, your messages or anything else.

## How it is used

Your comment is checked against the reel's keyword. If it matches, you get one DM and possibly a public reply. To avoid sending the same message twice, the app stores your username, the comment text and ID, and whether the message was sent.

## Sharing

This data is not sold or shared with anyone. It is stored with Supabase, which hosts the app's database, and is used only for the purpose above.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Stored comment records are kept only to prevent duplicate messages. To have yours deleted, send a direct message to @[YOUR_INSTAGRAM_HANDLE] on Instagram saying "Delete my data", and it will be removed within 30 days. Deleting your comment on Instagram also stops any further use of it.

## Contact

Send a direct message to @[YOUR_INSTAGRAM_HANDLE] on Instagram.
